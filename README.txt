মেলা — FESTIVAL DE BANGLADESH 2026
Screen display for the projector / TV / LED banner
Comunidade de Bangladesh do Porto

-------------------------------------------------------------------
RUNNING IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click  index.html  . It opens in your browser.
Check the list at the top ("From this folder"), then press "Start show".

Use Chrome if you have it. Safari and Firefox work too.

At the venue: plug in the projector or TV FIRST, drag the browser
window onto that screen, then press Start show. It goes fullscreen
and asks for the microphone so the show moves to the music.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
KEYS DURING THE SHOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  S       show or hide the settings panel
  F       fullscreen on / off
  ->      jump to the next scene
  <-      go back one scene
  Space   hold the current scene (press again to release)
  B       black screen — use it during a speech
  Esc     back to the setup page (nothing is lost)

The mouse pointer hides itself after 3 seconds.

THE TWO MOVING BACKGROUNDS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Behind the big logo the music screen now runs a laser tunnel — shapes
rushing out past the edges, like a DJ visual. They are not all the same:
wide hexagons, rings cut into separate bars, turned squares, triangles,
and loose shapes drifting between them, spaced out so the screen stays
open around the logo. It moves with the music: the rings widen and brighten on the
bass, the beams flicker on the top end, and every beat lifts the whole
picture a little. There is no hard strobe — it stays comfortable for a
room full of people.

The old green-and-red visualiser bars are gone from the whole show. The
tunnel replaced them, and the logo, the flags and the sponsor row still
beat with the music on their own scenes.

There are three of them, one per screen, and all three move to the music
the same way:

  Music screen   the laser tunnel — rings and beams rushing outwards
  Second screen  a lattice rolling like cloth, wide arcs swinging round
                 the logo, confetti rising off the floor
  Music only     a corridor of corner brackets with streaks tearing past
                 and chevrons opening out
  Laser show     a tunnel of lit tiles, full screen, with sweeping beams
  Liquid         mirrored colour flowing over the screen
  Kaleidoscope   petals opening and closing round the middle
  Ribbons        bands of light rolling across the screen
  Fireworks      shells going up on the beat over a night sky
  Neon grid      a floor running to the horizon under a banded sun

The two flag screens use the real flag artwork from media/brand — 
flag-bd.jpg and flag-pt.jpg. Replace either file with your own and the
waving screen and the "Two flags" card both pick it up. If a file is
missing the show falls back to a flag it draws itself, so nothing breaks.
They keep the real flag colours, so the colour presets below do not
touch them. If you would rather use your own artwork, the "Two flags"
scene near the start still shows media/slides/flags.png.

The music-only screens carry no logo and no words at all — they are the ones to
leave up while the music is playing. They are spread through the running
order so the evening keeps changing; move them, shorten them or switch
any of them off in "Running order".

The newest ones — Laser show, Kaleidoscope, Ribbons and Neon grid — do
not run the same way for long. Every seven to fifteen seconds their loop turns
round and starts running the other way (it waits for a beat to do it and
eases through the turn), and every six to thirteen seconds their shapes
change over, part way through a scene: the petal count, the number of
bands, the lanes on the grid. The music screen, the second screen and
"Music only" are untouched and behave exactly as they always did.

Three choices cover both screens, on the setup page under "Screen &
look" → "Moving backgrounds", and also mid-show on the S panel:

  Neon           violet, amber-and-pink, and ice blue (default)
  Flag colours   green, amber and red on all three
  Pattern only   the two screens with a logo fall back to the orange
                 pattern, and "Music only" is skipped — with no moving
                 background there would be nothing on it

They only appear on those screens. Every other scene is unchanged.

COLOURS FOR THE MUSIC-ONLY SCREENS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The seven screens with no logo and no words — Music only, Laser show,
Liquid, Kaleidoscope, Ribbons, Fireworks and Neon grid — can all be
recoloured at once. Setup page, "Screen & look" → "Colours — the
music-only screens", and the same buttons on the S panel during the
show. The change shows on the very next frame, so you can switch while
one of them is on screen.

  Each its own   the colours each screen was drawn with (default)
  Bangladesh     green, amber and red
  Rainbow        the whole spectrum
  Ice            blue, cyan and white
  Sunset         orange, pink and gold
  Jungle         green, lime and teal
  Candy          pink, violet and cyan

The backgrounds go with it — the dark ground under each screen takes
its tint from whichever preset is up.

The music screen and the second screen are not affected: they keep the
festival's own colours so the logo and the lettering always sit right.
Those two follow "Moving backgrounds" as before.

ADDING OR CHANGING PICTURES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Just drop files into the right folder and name them in order.
Then press "Rescan folder" on the setup page — or reload the page.

  media/porto/            porto-1.jpg, porto-2.jpg, porto-3.jpg …
  media/bangladesh/       bd-1.jpg, bd-2.jpg, bd-3.jpg …
  media/sponsors/         sponsor-1.png, sponsor-2.png …
  media/slides/extra/     extra-1.png, extra-2.png …   (full-screen
                          designed cards, shown one after another)

Gaps in the numbering are fine. jpg, jpeg, png and webp all work.

Single fixed files:

  media/slides/opening.png    the main banner, shown first
  media/slides/flags.png      the two-flags artwork
  media/slides/closing.png    the thank-you card, shown last. Delete it
                              and the show draws its own instead.
  media/brand/flag-bd.jpg     the Bangladesh flag artwork
  media/brand/flag-pt.jpg     the Portuguese flag artwork
  media/brand/logo.png        small logo in the top-right corner
  media/brand/background.png  pattern behind the designed slides

Replace any of these by overwriting the file with the same name.
If one of them is missing the show falls back to a coded version
of that scene, so nothing breaks.

Photo size: around 2400 pixels wide is plenty. Very large files
(5 MB and up) can make the laptop stutter on a long evening.

If you have a picture you would rather not rename, open
media/manifest.js and add its path to the right list.

CHANGING THE WORDS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything on screen is editable on the setup page, under
"Words on screen" — the welcome, the second screen's headline and the
line under it, the programme times, the thank-you card, the notice, and
the line along the bottom. Nothing needs to be edited in code.

THE LED WALL AT THE VENUE (5 m WIDE, 3 m HIGH)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
That wall is 5:3, a little squarer than an ordinary 16:9 screen. The
show fills whatever shape it is given, so there is nothing to change —
but a few things are worth knowing on the night.

  * Drag the browser window onto the wall and press Start show. It goes
    fullscreen and the whole show reshapes itself to 5:3.

  * Ask the LED company what resolution their processor takes (5 m x 3 m
    on the usual panels is around 1280 x 768) and set the laptop to send
    that, one pixel to one pixel, with no scaling. Anything the wall has
    to rescale will soften the lettering.

  * Edge margin can stay at 0. LED walls do not cut the edges the way
    old TVs and projectors do. If the panels turn out not to fill the
    whole frame, raise it a little.

  * The main banner, the two flags and the thank-you card are 16:9
    artwork. On a 5:3 wall the show fills the screen with them and loses
    about 6% off each side — nothing important sits there. If you would
    rather see the whole card with the pattern around it, remake that
    file 5:3 (for example 2500 x 1500).

  * To see it in the wall's shape before the night, open "Screen & look"
    on the setup page and press "Open a 5 x 3 window". A window opens in
    that shape — press Start show inside it and it is exactly what the
    wall will show. (Allow pop-ups if the browser blocks it.)

-------------------------------------------------------------------
IF THE SCREEN CROPS THE EDGES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Some TVs and projectors cut off the outer few percent of the picture.
Open "Screen & look" and raise "Edge margin" until nothing is lost.
You can also do it mid-show: press S, then move "Edge margin".

The designed slides fill the screen edge to edge. On an ordinary
16:9 or 16:10 screen that costs nothing — the banner has margin
around its artwork. If the screen turns out to be a very different
shape, like a long LED strip, filling it would cut the sponsor row
off, so the show notices and fits the whole card on the pattern
background instead. Nothing to set.
